# Service Catalogue

All pricing below is a working commercial hypothesis for validation. Figures are ex GST and should be adjusted after vendor quotes, insurance costs and actual delivery effort are known.

## 1. Operations Risk & Waste Review

**Purpose:** paid diagnostic and qualification product.

**Indicative price:** $2,500–$4,500.

### Includes
- Microsoft 365 / identity review;
- device and endpoint review;
- backup and recovery review;
- licence and SaaS waste review;
- basic cyber posture review;
- business continuity dependencies;
- high-level process-waste interviews;
- identification of obvious automation opportunities;
- prioritised 90-day remediation roadmap.

### Customer outputs
- risk summary;
- technology waste summary;
- automation opportunity summary;
- recommended remediation roadmap;
- estimated project and recurring operating model.

### Commercial rule
If the customer signs an agreed SecureOps onboarding project within a defined period, some or all of the review fee may be credited against onboarding.

- Microsoft 365 administration;
- Entra identity administration;

- patching and endpoint security management;
- joiner/mover/leaver workflow;
- licence administration;
- backup monitoring;
- vendor coordination;
- asset lifecycle administration;

**Purpose:** managed operations plus enhanced cyber assurance and evidence.

**Indicative price:** $225/user/month.

**Indicative minimum:** $4,500/month.

### Adds to SecureOps 365
- continuous vulnerability-management process;
- security awareness / phishing programme;
- MDR/SOC partner service where commercially viable;
- enhanced recovery testing;
- privileged-access review;
- security-control evidence pack;
- cyber-insurance evidence support;
- monthly security posture reporting;
- Essential Eight-oriented gap tracking where appropriate.

### Positioning boundary
Do not describe this as an ASD/ACSC certification. Formal maturity claims require an appropriate assessment process.

## 6. Automation Sprint

**Purpose:** remove a specific, measurable business-process bottleneck.

**Indicative project price:** $7,500–$25,000.

### Method
1. Baseline the current process.
2. Calculate labour/time cost.
3. Define measurable success criteria.
4. Design the automated workflow.
5. Add human approval where risk requires it.
6. Implement using the least-complex suitable platform.
7. Measure before/after performance.
8. Document ownership and failure handling.

### Likely platforms
- Power Automate;
- Copilot Studio;
- Azure Logic Apps;
- Azure Functions;
- Microsoft 365 Copilot where justified;
- APIs from existing business systems.

### Optional recurring management
$600–$2,500/month depending on workflow count, business criticality and support requirements.

## 7. FieldOps / Regional Resilience

**Purpose:** support distributed, field-based and regional operations.

**Indicative add-on:** +$35/user/month plus approximately $750/site/month as a starting hypothesis.

### Includes
- remote-site technology standards;
- primary/secondary connectivity design and monitoring;
- managed networking;
- cellular/LEO backup where appropriate;
- remote endpoint management;
- critical-spares plan;
- UPS/network resilience planning;
- field-service partner coordination;
- site technology documentation.

### Excludes
Carrier, satellite, hardware, cabling and third-party field-service charges unless specifically bundled.

## Packaging principle

Customers should buy outcomes rather than a long technical menu.

The preferred expansion path is:

**Review → Remediation → SecureOps → Assured/Vertical Add-on → Automation → Ongoing Improvement**

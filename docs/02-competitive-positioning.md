# Competitive Positioning

## Market reality

The Australian MSP market is already crowded with providers offering combinations of:

- managed IT;
- Microsoft 365;
- cybersecurity;
- Essential Eight-aligned services;
- cloud;
- backup;
- Power Platform automation;
- AI/Copilot advisory;
- mining and remote-site support.

Therefore the company should not rely on the words **managed IT + cyber + AI** as differentiation.

## Representative competitor patterns observed

### Melbourne

Providers commonly publish per-user managed service pricing and package Microsoft 365 administration, support, backup and Essential Eight-oriented security. This confirms that generic SMB managed IT is already highly productised and price-comparable.

### Brisbane and Central Queensland

More sophisticated providers combine managed IT, Microsoft cloud, Power Platform, AI, cybersecurity and distributed operations. Some explicitly address mining supplier security questionnaires and quantify AI benefits in labour hours or dollars.

### Perth

The resources market is comparatively mature. Multiple providers position around mining, Microsoft 365, Essential Eight, AI governance, remote sites and compliance-heavy managed services.

### Newcastle / Hunter

Industrial and regional providers already combine managed IT, cybersecurity, AI/automation and mining/industrial support.

## Implication

Competing as "another good MSP" creates price pressure and weak differentiation.

The defendable wedge should instead combine:

1. **Operational ownership** — run the customer's digital workplace.
2. **Evidence** — continuously maintain proof of security and operational controls.
3. **Industry workflows** — understand the business process that creates the requirement.
4. **Measurable improvement** — quantify waste removed, risk reduced and work automated.

## Working positioning

> **Run securely. Prove it. Improve continuously.**

Alternative explanatory statement:

> SecureOps 365 provides managed digital operations for Australian businesses that have outgrown ordinary IT support. We run the workplace, maintain the security evidence and automate work that should no longer be manual.

## Vertical positioning

### Professional services

> Turn regulatory and client requirements into secure, repeatable technology workflows.

### Mining / METS

> Win and retain contracts without scrambling to prove your IT security.

### Automation

> Show us the repetitive work. We will quantify what it costs, determine whether automation is justified, and measure the result.

### Regional / field operations

> Keep distributed teams connected, secure and operational when a site, carrier or device fails.

## What not to claim

- Do not claim ASD or ACSC "certification" for Essential Eight unless a specific recognised certification basis exists.
- Do not provide legal conclusions about AML/CTF compliance.
- Do not represent business-IT controls as equivalent to OT/SCADA security.
- Do not claim automation ROI before establishing the customer's baseline.
- Do not claim complete regulatory compliance through a technology service alone.

## Defensibility strategy

The long-term moat should be built from:

- repeatable onboarding and remediation runbooks;
- sector-specific security evidence packs;
- automation templates;
- integrations with customer systems;
- benchmark data across comparable customers;
- measurable improvement history;
- eventually, a proprietary customer-facing operations and assurance dashboard.

Technology expertise alone is not a durable moat. Operational knowledge and embedded workflows are harder to replace.

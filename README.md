# SecureOps 365

**Managed Digital Operations for Australian SMEs**

SecureOps 365 is a working business concept for an Australian technology services company focused on organisations that have outgrown traditional break/fix IT support but are not large enough to build a complete internal technology, cybersecurity, compliance and automation function.

The working positioning is:

> **Run securely. Prove it. Improve continuously.**

The model combines four capabilities:

1. **Operate** — Microsoft 365, users, devices, cloud, networking, support and lifecycle management.
2. **Protect** — cybersecurity controls, monitoring, backup, recovery and security improvement.
3. **Assure** — evidence for cyber insurance, customer/supplier questionnaires, regulatory workflows and contractual requirements.
4. **Improve** — automation, AI-enabled workflows and measurable reduction of repetitive work.

## Current strategy

The current market thesis is to avoid launching as a generic MSP. The initial commercial focus is on Australian organisations with approximately **20–100 employees**, with expansion toward 150 employees where the operating model remains suitable.

Priority segments under validation:

- Professional services: accounting, legal, conveyancing, property and engineering firms.
- Mining/METS contractors and resource-sector suppliers.
- Construction and field-service organisations.
- Regional and distributed businesses requiring resilient connectivity and remote operations.
- Healthcare and other regulated SMEs as a later-stage vertical once delivery maturity is higher.

Regional Australia is treated as a **delivery architecture** rather than a vertical. Mining OT/SCADA is explicitly outside the initial direct service boundary and should be handled through qualified specialist partners.

## Current phase

**Phase: Market validated enough to begin structured customer validation.**

The next objective is not to build proprietary SaaS. It is to validate willingness to pay, win initial customers, document repeated operational problems, and use those patterns to determine what should later become proprietary software.

The intended progression is:

**Specialised consulting → Productised remediation → Recurring managed operations → Industry workflow IP → Proprietary platform**
